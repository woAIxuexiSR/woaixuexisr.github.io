--- layout: notes_base --- {%- comment -%} notes_listing.html — shared listing layout for the Paper Notes Database. Used by every Conference_Page, Category_Page, and Tag_Page (Requirements 7.1, 8.1, 9.1). The generator (notes_generator.rb -> add_listing_page) assigns each of these pages `layout: notes_listing` and populates `page.data` with: * page.title — the page/document title * page.heading — the human-readable heading * page.listing_type — "conference", "category", or "tag" * page.papers — the pre-filtered array of Paper_Index entries Layout: 1. A heading + a statistics summary (total, Conference/Journal split, and — for conference pages — a per-category breakdown). 2. A preview of the first N List_Items with a "Show more" control that progressively reveals the rest client-side (assets/notes/listing.js). All papers are present in the DOM (Requirements 7.1/8.1/9.1); the extra ones are just visually collapsed until expanded. Empty-state mutual exclusivity (Requirements 7.3/7.4, 8.3/8.4, 9.3/9.4; Property 11): the if/else guarantees the list and the `.notes-empty` message are never both rendered. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign papers = page.papers -%} {%- assign paper_count = papers | size -%} {%- assign heading = page.heading | default: page.title -%} {%- assign preview_limit = 10 -%}
